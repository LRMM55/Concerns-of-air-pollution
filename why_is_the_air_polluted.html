Why is the Air Polluted?

Air pollution is caused by smoke, harmful gases, and chemicals released into the atmosphere.

Main Causes

Vehicles such as cars, buses, motorcycles, and trucks release harmful gases into the air every day.

Factories and power plants burn fossil fuels to produce energy, creating smoke and toxic chemicals.

Burning garbage and plastic also pollutes the atmosphere.

Deforestation increases pollution because trees naturally absorb carbon dioxide and clean the air.

  • Vehicle smoke
  • Factory emissions
  • Burning garbage
  • Deforestation
